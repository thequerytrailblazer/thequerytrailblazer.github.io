This project analyzes the ELO rank longevity (how long a person hold top 20 ELO rank) of top-tier Chess Grandmasters over a 20-year span. 

## Key Insights
* **Peak Dominance:** Visualizing historic rating ceilings (2850+ ELO).
* **Longevity Analysis:** Identifying players maintaining Top-20 status across decades.
